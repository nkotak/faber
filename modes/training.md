# Mode: training — Training Evaluation

For each course or certification the candidate asks about, evaluate across 6 dimensions:

| Dimension | What it measures |
|-----------|------------------|
| North Star alignment | Does it move the candidate closer to or further from their target? |
| Recruiter signal | What do hiring managers think when they see this on a CV? |
| Time and effort | Weeks × hours/week |
| Opportunity cost | What can't the candidate do during this time? |
| Risks | Outdated content? Weak brand? Too basic? |
| Portfolio deliverable | Does it produce a demonstrable artifact? |

## Verdicts

- **DO IT** → 4-12 week plan with weekly deliverables and scoreboard
- **DON'T DO IT** → a better alternative with justification
- **DO IT WITH A TIMEBOX** (max X weeks) → condensed plan, essentials only

## Priority

Training that improves credibility in "production-grade AI":
1. Evals and LLM testing
2. Observability and monitoring
3. Cost/reliability trade-offs
4. AI governance and safety
5. Enterprise AI architecture
