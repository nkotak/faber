Each entry has the structure:

```

- **Observed in:** [application num]s [num1, num2, ...]
- **Outcome:** [Interview / Offer]
- **Pattern:** [description of the prose pattern]
- **Apply when:** [drafting guidance]
- **First seen:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
- **Confirmation count:** [N]
```
